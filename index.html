Field of Thorns

Grow a maze of thorns and hold the gate until the swarm is broken. Clear every map to unlock the next difficulty.

Towers cost gold. Every slime you defeat drops gold, and Markets pay bonus gold after every cleared round. Some rounds bring mini-bosses and bosses that cost several lives, and every 7th round flies over your maze. How long the swarm lasts? Nobody knows.

Picking a card again levels it up (max 10). Levels 3 and 6 give a bonus step, MAX gives a double bonus — the tall pips. The timer is paused while you choose.
