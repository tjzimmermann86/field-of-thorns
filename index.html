Field of Thorns

Grow a maze of thorns, hold the gate for 50 rounds. Clear every map to unlock the next difficulty.
Difficulty
Map

Every 3rd round brings mini-bosses (2 lives), every 10th a boss (3–4 lives), rounds 25 and 50 a giant boss (5 lives). Every 7th round flies over your maze, so keep some anti-air ready. Beating round 25 grants a rare card.

Picking a card again levels it up (max 10). Levels 3 and 6 give a bonus step, MAX gives a double bonus — the tall pips. The timer is paused while you choose.
