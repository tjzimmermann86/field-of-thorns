Field of Thorns

Grow a maze of thorns and hold the gate until the swarm is broken. Two ways to play: the story campaign or the challenge maps.
StoryThe Captive QueenOne big land. Conquer the Slime King's camps one by one and free the Queen from his tower.
ChallengeHold the GatePick a difficulty and a map, then survive 50 rounds. Clear every map to unlock the next difficulty.
Difficulty
Map

You start with Arrows and Thorns; every other tower is researched once with gold. Every slime you defeat drops gold, and Markets pay bonus gold after every cleared round. Some rounds bring mini-bosses and bosses that cost several lives, and every 7th round flies over your maze. Hold out for 50 rounds to break the swarm.

Ranking

Round cleared — pick a card

Picking a card again levels it up (max 10). Levels 3 and 6 give a bonus step, MAX gives a double bonus — the tall pips. The timer is paused while you choose.
